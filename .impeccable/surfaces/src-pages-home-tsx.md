# KinoAz / Milli Pərdə: web app (Home, Films, Film detail, Directors and Actors)

Mode: Operate (find a film, open it on YouTube). Pinned by the user: the five Stitch screens built from the user's prompts (project 15778261104332329369). Reference exports: `.impeccable/reference/stitch/`.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Finding an Azerbaijani film and starting it is one click: every film card opens the film on YouTube in a new tab. It refuses the "streaming clone with fake stats" default: no ratings, watchlists, or view counts, because none exist.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black cinema room (#131316, #0e0e11) with one saturated cinema red (#e50914) used for the primary action and active state. Bebas Neue condensed uppercase for titles, Plus Jakarta Sans for reading. 8px radii, 2:3 poster cards, play-on-hover overlay, circular monogram initials in red gradient instead of portraits.

STORY: A visitor understands this is a catalogue of real Azerbaijani films, picks one by era, genre or name, and watches it on the official YouTube upload. They believe it because each card is a verified YouTube link and the page says honestly when it is only a trailer, fragment or search.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed featured film (Nəsimi) with left scrim, Bebas title at ~96px, year, genre and runtime chips, synopsis, red "YouTube-da izlə" primary button and outlined "Ətraflı" secondary. Fixed transparent-to-dark header with logo, four links, search. Era jump-pills sit just below the fold.

FORM: Pinned whole by the user's Stitch design (seed: none (pinned) "Cinematic dark Netflix-like, black plus red, five screens from the user's prompts").

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
